# Orbit Trademark Policy

The Orbit **code** is free software under the [GNU AGPL v3](LICENSE). The Orbit **name, logo and visual identity** are not covered by that license. They identify the official project, so that people always know what they are getting.

This policy explains what you can and cannot do with them. It follows the approach of projects such as Mozilla, WordPress and Mastodon.

## What the marks are

- The name **Orbit** when it refers to this software, its desktop app or Orbit Runtime.
- The Orbit **logo**: the purple planet with a ring and two eyes, in any color or size (`build/icon.svg`, `build/icon.png`).
- The **banner** and the companion-planet artwork used to present the project (`docs/assets/`).

## Always allowed

- Saying that your work is **based on Orbit**, **compatible with Orbit** or **a fork of Orbit**.
- Using the name to talk about the project: articles, tutorials, videos, talks, reviews.
- Using the logo to link to the official project or its repository.
- Building, running and sharing **unmodified** copies of the official source under the official name.
- Keeping the name and logo in a fork you develop in order to submit changes back to this project.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Distributing a **modified** version under the name Orbit, or with the Orbit logo as its icon.
- Using the name or logo for a **product, service or hosted offering** (paid or free), for example "Orbit Cloud" or "Orbit Pro".
- Using the name or logo in a way that suggests your project, company or service is **official, endorsed or affiliated** when it is not.
- Using a name or logo that is **confusingly similar** to Orbit's in the same field.
- Using the marks in a domain name, app store listing or social account in a way that could be mistaken for the official project.

## If you publish a fork

Forks are welcome; that is what the license is for. Please:

1. Give it a **different name** and a **different icon**.
2. Say clearly that it is a fork, for example: *"Based on Orbit, by Thomas Boda. Not affiliated with the Orbit project."*
3. Keep the original copyright notices, as the AGPL requires.

## Questions

Not sure whether your use is fine? Open an issue on this repository before you publish, and we will answer quickly. Permission is granted case by case and in writing.

This policy may change as the project grows.
